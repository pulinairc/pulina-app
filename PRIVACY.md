# Lobby privacy policy

Updated 11.11.2026.

Lobby has no accounts and no servers of its own. It collects nothing about you: no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

## What leaves your device

Only what you set up, and only to the place you pick:

- **Chat:** your nick and messages go to the IRC servers you connect to, or to your own lobby-server.
- **Link previews:** when a message holds a link, Lobby loads that page to show its title and picture.
- **Files you share:** go to the storage you choose in Settings, such as catbox.moe, your own server, S3 or R2, or SFTP.
- **Now playing:** if you add a Last.fm username, Lobby asks Last.fm what that account is playing.
- **Updates:** the desktop apps check GitHub for a newer version.
- **Reports:** reporting a message opens your own mail app with the report. Nothing is sent unless you send that mail.

## What stays on your device

Settings, chat history, people's photos and colours you set, and blocked people stay on your device, or on your lobby-server if you use one.
